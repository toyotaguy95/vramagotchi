The rest of the board

    What is a VRAMagotchi?

    A small pixel pet that sits above your prompt in Claude Code. Every token Claude writes is food.

    It hatches from an egg on your first prompt. It naps when you stop typing, gets stuffed when the context window fills up, and faints when it runs out.

    Feed it for long enough and it stops being a blob.

    Get a pet

    Type this inside Claude Code. You get an egg, and your next prompt hatches it.

    /plugin install vramagotchi --marketplace toyotaguy95/vramagotchi

    Then put it on this board. Nothing is sent until you do this.

    /pet board join

    How the board counts

    • A belly holds one day of food. A pet's score grows by at most 1M tokens a day, however much it claims to have eaten.
    • The board keeps its own count. Days fed and streaks run on the board's clock, so they cannot be made up.
    • Ties go to whoever joined first. Feeding your pet every day beats everything else.
    • First place wears the crown. That is the whole prize. There is nothing here worth money.
    • What a pet sends: its name, how much it ate, and what it owns. Never your code or your prompts.
